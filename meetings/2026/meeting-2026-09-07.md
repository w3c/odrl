### 3. Digital Policy Working Group Charter
The meeting was mostly devoted to this [draft charter](https://w3c.github.io/odrl/w3c-wg-draft-charter-2026/dpwg-draft-charter.html).

**Decisions**
- Keep the broad title "Digital Policy Working Group". The wider framing
  attracts more communities and other W3C groups, and outweighs the more
  narrowly technical nature of the work.
- Add a dedicated section for the Primer and the Best Practices document,
  so that they are prioritised rather than left to the end.
- Add a DPV profile to the tentative deliverables as a quick win.
- The Community Group has up to two weeks to comment before the draft is
  handed back to W3C for internal processing.

**Deliverables**
- Five normative specifications, with "ODRL Protocol Architecture"
  possibly renamed "Architecture and Protocols".
- Tentative: market data profile and data spaces profile.
- Other: use cases, test suites, and a possible note on policy
  visualisation.
- Under consideration: a profile covering ethical aspects of systems,
  based on work with GAIA-X Spain.

**Open questions**
- Whether "compliance" or "conformance" is the right term for evaluation
  reports.
- Yassir: clarify whether "policy" covers only access and usage control,
  or also broader regulatory compliance.
- Andrea: consider merging the semantic model, the conformance report and
  the state of the world into one ontology, or a closely linked set, to
  avoid fragmentation.
- Joaquín: the architecture section should allow for several
  architectures (zero trust among them) and several protocols.
