[1] Daham's comments
Our position, in four points:

    Operators select concepts according to the hierarchy declared by the profile. typeOf means at or below a concept; superClassOf means at or above it. ODRL already has this pair for parts (isPartOf, hasPart); taxonomies need the same pair.
    Exclusion is the complement. To exclude a concept and everything below it, use the complement of typeOf ("not at or below"). Strict variants need no new operators: typeOf together with neq.
    The profile defines the relationship. It states whether the hierarchy comes from subclass relations, SKOS broader, or part-whole relations in the external vocabulary, and the reasoner follows that relation.
    Missing information is not automatically negative. If the vocabulary does not establish a link, our reasoner returns Unknown rather than treating the constraint as not satisfied.

We have a formal account of this with a benchmark over DPV purposes and GeoNames; https://github.com/Daham-Mustaf/odrl-grounded-benchmark.
If it helps for ODRL 3.0, I can open one dedicated issue that states these four points as a requirement, separate from the two threads above.
