# ODRL3 Charter

## Scope
- Formalise **ODRL 3.0** as the successor to the 2.2 Information Model and Vocabulary, prioritising a **normative evaluation semantics** (the most consistently requested gap across literature, GitHub issues and mailing list)

- Establish a **governance process for vocabulary growth**: a living, versioned registry (SKOS-based, as ODRL 2.2 already is) rather than terms frozen to the Recommendation's publication cycle

- Define the **evaluation architecture**: state of the world representation, request/response format, gap resolution strategy (Closed World Assumption), broadly analogous to established access control reference architectures (e.g. XACML's PDP/PEP/PIP separation)

- Define a **conformance model** covering both policies and processors (validators, evaluators, monitors, etc.), with a real-world test protocol, not just a static test suite

- Treat **profiles as the primary extension mechanism** for domain needs (AI, data spaces, finance, data quality, legal, security) so the core stays stable while domains iterate independently

- Ensure **cross-standard alignment** is explicitly in scope, not an afterthought: bindings to DPV, DCAT, DID and Verifiable Credentials, so assets, policies and identities can be connected across W3C specifications

- Consider **provenance** as a first-class concern for policies and their evaluation, not only for the assets they govern

- Out of scope (tentative): building or mandating specific enforcement infrastructure; legal interpretation of policy content; competing with or replacing non-ODRL policy languages (only alignment, not replacement)


## Deliverables
- **Use Cases and Requirements** (Note)
- **Primer** (Note). A gentle introduction to newcomers.
- **Policy Model** (Recommendation). Core model, represented with an ontology. This model (or some other document) must define "how to write a profile".
- **Evaluation** (Recommendation). Formal semantics document.
- **Architecture and Protocols** (Recommendation) Components are defined, as well as their interoperation.
- **Conformance and test suite** (Note)
- **Visualisation** (Note)
- **Vocabulary Registries** (Registry)

Out of the charter:
- **Profiles** (e.g. AI, data, finance, data quality, legal data space connection, W3C DID and VC, ), maintained alongside the core

Coordination:
